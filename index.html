The Day Our World Began

On Sunday, October 25, 2025 at 4:20 PM, heaven sent us our sweetest gift.

Looking into those peaceful eyes, we knew life would never be the same. Three hundred and sixty-five days have passed in the blink of an eye, each filled with his quiet breathing, his first gentle smile, his playful claps, and shouting "Amma"—growing into a joyful 10kg boy who fills our hearts with unconditional love.
